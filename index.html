Questions

Before you download

What is a bedtime alarm?

Most alarms wake you up. A bedtime alarm tells you when to go to bed. TuckIn works out tonight's bedtime from your strain, sleep debt and wake-up time, then rings at that time, so the plan doesn't depend on you watching the clock.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

TuckIn reads sleep and heart-rate data from Apple Health, so you need a wearable that writes those to Health. Apple Watch does. Many other trackers do too, if their app syncs to Apple Health.

How is this different from my sleep tracker?

Your tracker tells you how you slept. TuckIn tells you what to do today so tonight goes well. It uses the data your tracker already collects, so they work together.

How long before the bedtime is personal to me?

TuckIn builds your baseline from the last 45 days in Apple Health and needs at least 7 nights of 4 hours or more. If you already wear a tracker, that's usually there on day one.

What does it cost?

TuckIn is free to download on the App Store.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 18.2 or later. Some features, like on-device sleep summaries, depend on your iPhone model and iOS version.

Is this medical advice?

No. TuckIn is a general wellness app, not a medical device. If you think you have a sleep disorder, talk to a doctor.
